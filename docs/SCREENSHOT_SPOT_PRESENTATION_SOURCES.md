# Screenshot spot presentation

Checked on 2026-09-13. The source screenshots identify the requested zones. The
new profiles retain separate Dehkia stages, Elvia variants, and three Floodlands
locations. The generic Ash Forest profile has no stage-specific AP cap.
The generic Winter Tree Fossil profile likewise omits the cap until the user
confirms the supported 280-AP variant, whose trash is also used by the 250-AP zone.

Regions and AP caps come from Garmoth's current
[Monster Zone AP Caps](https://garmoth.com/grind-tracker/monster-ap-caps).
The app treats these as total AP caps; they are not equipment-sheet AP or
minimum entry requirements. Unverified AP/DP and crystal recommendations remain
absent. The detail view displays the confirmed cap independently.

Gavinya's total 1,990 AP / 820 DP recommendation, 2,020 cap and knockdown/bound
control effects come from the official
[July 30, 2026 update](https://blackdesert.pearlabyss.com/TR/en-Us/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=19633).
The current [September 10, 2026 update](https://www.naeu.playblackdesert.com/en-US/News/Detail?countryType=en-US&groupContentNo=10577)
removed Gavinya from the highest-tier list, so its new profile omits that tag.

Star's End uses the total 1,920 AP / 800 DP recommendation, 1,950 cap and
stun/stiffness/freeze recommendation documented in
[Garmoth's remake guide](https://garmoth.com/guides/post/stars-end-remake).
Its listed Marni's Realm and Allan Serbin's Landscape features are retained.
No highest-tier tag is inferred from that older guide.

All new emblem assets depict the zone's primary trash item, as in the supplied
screenshots. Exact asset URLs and checksums are recorded in
[screenshot-spots-sources.json](../data/spot-icons/screenshot-spots-sources.json).
Images are only converted to PNG, with no recoloring or generated artwork.
Where no verified scene was available, cards use the app's neutral background.
The three Floodlands locations share the already verified Floodlands scene.

Black Desert game assets belong to Pearl Abyss. Garmoth and BDO Codex do not
endorse this project.

What's new in Grindcrest 1.15.0:

• Automatic grind detection quietly pauses when Black Desert is minimized and can resume on the next recognized drop.
• Rotation messages are recognized in English, German, French and Spanish, with a verified rotation profile for Zephyros.
• Pauses appear as gaps in session timelines and overlays and remain visible after a session is restored.
• Share images focus on loot and hourly figures: trash loot comes first, followed by rare drops and other items. Clicking outside closes the preview.
• Overlay drop grids adapt when resized, and the live demo shows a recorded Magaia session.
• Localization, navigation and the grind rating scale have been improved.

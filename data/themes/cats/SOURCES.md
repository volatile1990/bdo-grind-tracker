## kitten-lounge.png prompt

Create a production-quality transparent PNG illustration asset for a cozy cat-themed desktop game companion interface. Stylized storybook illustration, hand-painted gouache with delicate detailed fur, warm natural cream, ginger, taupe and soft sage colors, sophisticated cozy aesthetic rather than neon or pink. Three extremely cute small kittens cuddling: a cream kitten dozing with a paw over the cushion edge, a fluffy ginger kitten sitting awake and looking at the viewer, and a small gray tabby curled asleep. They sit close together on one low oatmeal woven linen cushion with a subtle stitched seam, a single small sage green ball of yarn nearby with a loose curved thread. Entire group fully visible, all ears, tails, whiskers and cushion within canvas, no cropped elements. Wide horizontal composition, approximately 3:2 ratio, illustration group fills most of canvas with modest transparent margin on all sides, no text, no letters, no logo, no UI mockup, no frame, no scenery, no floor plane, no background. TRUE alpha transparent background, crisp clean alpha edges without white matte or checkerboard. Lighting soft and warm from upper left, high quality polished charming kittens, discernible silhouettes when scaled to 230px wide, calm low contrast cushion details. Deliver only the isolated illustration, suitable to layer over a dark espresso user interface and use as a large decorative background corner.
